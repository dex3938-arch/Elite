[ 02 / THE RESORT ]

Not a place
to be found.

Elite is a private island resort at the quiet edge of the atoll. Days unfold without agenda and the ocean writes the only itinerary.

Arrive by seaplane or sail in beyond the reef. In the hush of the palms you will find only eighteen villas, an unbroken house reef, and a team who knows when to disappear.
